From first trial to first contract

The pathway every Ennavizion player follows. Players move at their own pace, and families see where they are at each stage.

  1. Trial

    A free session with our coaches. You get written feedback within a week.

  2. Develop

    Join the program for your age. Train three to five times a week with a personal plan.

  3. Compete

    Play in the Ennavizion League. Every match is filmed and added to your profile.

  4. Get seen

    Showcase days and a scouting profile shared with partner clubs at home and abroad.
