Community & social impact

Ennavizion's community work aims to open the door for talented players who couldn't otherwise afford coaching, kit or a route into competitive football.

Football shouldn't depend on what you can afford

Talent is everywhere. Good coaching, decent boots and a competitive league often aren't. Our community work exists to close that gap.

Through scholarships, equipment support and outreach, the aim is to give promising players from disadvantaged backgrounds the same start as anyone else at the academy.
