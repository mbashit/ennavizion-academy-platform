Community & social impact

Ennavizion is more than a football academy. We work to open the door for talented players who couldn't otherwise afford coaching, kit or a route into competitive football.

Football shouldn't depend on what you can afford

Talent is everywhere. Good coaching, decent boots and a competitive league often aren't. Our community program exists to close that gap.

Through scholarships, equipment support and outreach sessions, we aim to give promising players from disadvantaged backgrounds the same start as anyone else at the academy.
