About Ennavizion

A youth football academy set up to give young players the coaching, competition and support they need to go as far as their talent takes them.

Why the academy exists

Talent is everywhere. Good coaching, organised competition and a fair chance to be seen often aren't.

Too many gifted young players never get proper coaching, never play in an organised league, and never get in front of the people who could help them take the next step. Ennavizion brings those three things together in one place, for players aged 6 to 18.

Mission

We provide young players with accessible, high-quality football coaching, safe training environments, measurable development plans, competitive experience and opportunities to reach their potential.

Vision

To become a leading Nigerian youth football development platform, creating structured pathways from grassroots participation to higher levels of education, competition and sporting opportunity.

Questions parents ask

Anything else, send us a message.

Does my child need experience?

No. Players are placed in a group that suits their age and level after a trial session.

How much does it cost?

Monthly fees depend on the programme and are listed on the programmes page, along with the one-time registration fee. The first trial session is free.

What should players bring?

Football boots, shin pads, a water bottle and comfortable training wear.

How do scouts see players?

Through competitive matches and showcase opportunities. Any information shared with a club or scout about a player needs a parent or guardian's consent first. No academy can guarantee that a player will be selected, and we never promise it.
