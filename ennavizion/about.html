About Ennavizion

A youth football academy founded to give young players the coaching, competition and visibility they need to go as far as their talent takes them.

Our story

Ennavizion started with one pitch, twelve players and a simple idea: talent is everywhere, but opportunity isn't.

Too many gifted young players never get proper coaching, never play in an organised league, and never get in front of a scout. We built the academy to fix all three in one place.

Today we run programs at centres, organise our own youth league, and work with partner clubs who watch our players develop from the youngest age groups upward.

What we believe

Player first

Decisions are made for the player's long-term development, not for this weekend's result.

School matters

Players keep up with their education. We check school reports and offer homework time at every centre.

Safe to play

Every coach is vetted and trained in safeguarding. Parents always know who is working with their child.

Questions parents ask

Anything else, send us a message.

Does my child need experience?

No. Foundation groups welcome complete beginners. Older players are placed after a trial session.

How much does it cost?

Monthly fees depend on the program and are listed on the programs page. Scholarships are available for talented players who can't afford fees.

What should players bring?

Boots, shin pads, a water bottle and training wear. Academy kit is available in the shop.

How do scouts see players?

Through filmed league matches, termly showcase days and player profiles we share with partner clubs, always with parental consent.
