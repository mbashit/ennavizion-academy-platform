Safeguarding

Every player's safety and welfare comes before results.

If a child is in immediate danger

Call the emergency services on 112 straight away. Don't wait to contact the academy first.

Raising a concern

Contact the academy's safeguarding lead, [Safeguarding lead name], on [safeguarding phone number], or email with "Safeguarding" in the subject line. You can also use the contact form and choose "Safeguarding concern". You don't need to be sure something is wrong to raise it. Serious child-protection concerns follow this safeguarding process, not the ordinary enquiries queue.

Our safeguarding commitments

Ennavizion is committed to a safe, respectful and inclusive environment for every child taking part in its activities. All coaches, staff, volunteers and representatives must act in the best interests of children. The academy will:

  • designate a trained safeguarding lead and a clear reporting route;
  • check staff suitability and provide safeguarding training before anyone works with children;
  • keep appropriate supervision and attendance procedures;
  • set safe drop-off and collection arrangements;
  • prohibit abuse, harassment, bullying, exploitation and inappropriate punishment;
  • require staff to report safeguarding concerns promptly;
  • keep confidential safeguarding records with restricted access;
  • have procedures for emergencies and for escalating allegations to the appropriate authorities;
  • avoid inappropriate one-to-one private communication between adults and children;
  • require a parent or guardian's permission for photographs, videos and sharing information outside the academy.

Staff must never promise secrecy to a child who discloses a concern. The safeguarding lead coordinates the response, including referrals where necessary.

Photos and video

Permission to train is not permission to publish a child's image. Parents and guardians can give separate permission for internal coaching analysis, team or event photos, the website, social media, and controlled sharing with verified scouting contacts. Saying no to publication never affects a player's place in training, and permission can be withdrawn.

On this website

Applications for players under 18 can only be made by a parent or guardian, and children's details are visible only to academy staff signed in to a protected account. See the privacy notice.
