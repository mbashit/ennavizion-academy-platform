Safeguarding

Every player's safety and welfare comes before results.

If a child is in immediate danger

Call the emergency services on 112 straight away. Don't wait to contact the academy first.

Raising a concern with the academy

If you're worried about a player's safety or wellbeing, or about the behaviour of anyone working with players at Ennavizion, email with "Safeguarding" in the subject line, or use the contact form and choose "Safeguarding concern". You don't need to be sure something is wrong to raise it.

Our safeguarding policy

The academy's full safeguarding policy, including the name and contact details of its safeguarding lead, is being finalised and will be published on this page once approved. It will set out:

  • how coaches, staff and volunteers who work with players are vetted;
  • who the safeguarding lead is and how to reach them;
  • how concerns are recorded, handled and escalated, including to the relevant authorities;
  • codes of conduct for staff, players and parents;
  • how photos, video and information about players are handled, with parental consent.

On this website

Applications for players under 18 can only be made by a parent or guardian. Children's details are visible only to academy staff with an admin account, and medical notes are stored encrypted. See the privacy notice.
