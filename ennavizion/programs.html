Academy programs and fees

Four programs grouped by age and level. Every player starts with a free trial so we can place them in the right group.

Fees include coaching, league registration and termly progress reports. Kit is sold separately. Sibling discount: 10% off the second child.

Our approach

How a typical week of training is structured.

Technical

Ball mastery, first touch, passing range and finishing — the skills that open every door later.

Tactical

Small-sided games that teach players to scan, choose and act quickly, with video review for older groups.

Physical and mental

Age-appropriate strength and speed work, recovery habits, and coaching on confidence and focus.

Training centres

Train at the centre closest to you. Players can switch centres at the start of any term.

Other services

Beyond the academy programs, we offer:

  • Holiday camps during school breaks
  • One-to-one and small-group private coaching
  • Player video profiles and highlight reels
  • Coaching clinics for schools and community teams
