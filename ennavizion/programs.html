Choosing a programme

Which programme suits my child?

Choose the programme for the player's age. Goalkeepers can join the Goalkeeper Unit. Coaches confirm the right group at the trial, so you don't need to get it exactly right.

How to apply

A parent or guardian fills in a short online application. The academy reviews it and contacts you to confirm details and arrange a trial session.

Our coaching approach

What players work on across every programme.

Technical

Ball mastery, first touch, passing range and finishing: the skills that open every door later.

Tactical

Small-sided games that teach players to scan, choose and act quickly.

Physical and mental

Age-appropriate strength and speed work, recovery habits, and coaching on confidence and focus.
