Terms

Enrolment, fees, cancellations, contributions and use of this website.

Applying and enrolment

Submitting an application doesn't guarantee a place and doesn't commit you to pay anything. Applications are reviewed against availability, age eligibility and capacity, and the academy confirms decisions through its verified contact channels. Every new player's first trial session is free.

Fees

Programme fees are charged for the billing period shown on the programmes page. A one-time registration fee applies to newly admitted players. Any other charge is disclosed before you commit; transport, personal kit and external tournament costs aren't included in fees. Fee changes are announced before they take effect.

Cancellations and refunds

Tell the academy when a player is withdrawing. Cancellations made before a new paid training period begins may be eligible for a refund of unused prepaid fees, less any clearly disclosed lawful deductions. If the academy cancels a paid service without a suitable replacement, you'll be offered a remedy, including a refund where required. Ordinary missed sessions don't create a refund; make-up sessions may be offered where possible. Refunds are returned through a supported payment channel. Refund requests and complaints: or +7 906 590 93 21.

Contributions

Contributions are accepted only through the academy's verified payment routes and are only treated as received once the payment provider confirms them. You'll be told whether your contribution is restricted to a purpose you chose or can go where it's needed most. Contributions don't give access to information about supported players. Tax-deductible or registered-charity status is only stated if it has been legally established.

Shop orders

Sending a shop order from this website is a request, not a purchase. Availability, price, delivery or collection, and payment are confirmed with you before anything is paid.

Website content

We keep the information on this website as accurate as we can. Fixtures, programmes and other details can change; contact us if something matters to a decision you're making.
