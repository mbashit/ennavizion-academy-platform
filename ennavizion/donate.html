Support a future footballer

Help remove the financial barriers that stop talented young people from taking part in organised football.

Where contributions go

Contributions support training fees for players who can't afford them, essential equipment, transport help and community coaching.

    These show how support is planned to be used. They aren't a promise of a specific item or a named player. When you give, you choose whether your contribution goes where it's needed most or only toward a purpose you pick.

    How contributions are handled

    • A contribution is only recorded once Paystack has confirmed the payment.
    • If you choose a specific purpose, your contribution is kept for that purpose.
    • The academy keeps records of contributions, processing fees and spending, and will publish summaries once they have been reconciled and approved.
    • Supported players' private details and identifiable photos are never published without permission, and contributions don't give access to information about them.

    What happens when you give

    1. Choose

      Pick an amount and how it should be used.

    2. Pay securely

      You complete payment on Paystack's own checkout page.

    3. Confirmation

      You're brought back here once Paystack has confirmed the payment.
