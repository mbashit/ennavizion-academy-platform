Support a player

Your donation helps fund scholarships, kit, transport and community coaching for players who couldn't otherwise afford it.

What your donation supports

Every pledge goes toward one or more of the following, prioritised by need.

Scholarship support

Covers academy program fees for a player whose family can't afford them.

Equipment

Boots, shin pads, training wear and match kit.

Transport

Getting players to training sessions and match days.

Education

Supporting schoolwork alongside football, in line with our player-first approach.

Community projects

Outreach sessions and coaching access in underserved communities.
