Privacy notice

What this website collects, why, and how it is handled.

Ennavizion's full privacy policy is being prepared for professional review. Until it is published, this notice describes exactly what this website does with information. It is not a statement that any law has been fully complied with.

What we collect through this website

  • Trial applications, made by a parent or guardian: the player's name, date of birth, preferred position, current club and chosen programme, and the parent or guardian's name, phone number and email address. Where the site runs on the academy's own server, an optional medical or injury note can also be added; it is stored encrypted.
  • Contact messages: your name, email address, optional phone number, topic and message.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email address.
  • Donations, when online giving is open: your name, email address, amount and optional message. Card details are entered on Paystack's own payment page and never reach this website.

Why we use it

To handle the application or enquiry you send, to contact you about it, to keep players safe during sessions (medical notes), and to record donations. We don't sell personal information or use it for advertising.

How it is stored and who can see it

  • Submissions are stored with the website's host (Netlify) or, once it is in use, on the academy's own server. Either way they can only be viewed by academy staff signed in to a password-protected account.
  • Medical and injury notes are only collected on the academy's own server, where they are stored encrypted and separately from the rest of an application.
  • Information about a player is not shared with clubs or scouts without a parent or guardian's consent.

What stays in your browser

Shop cart items are kept in your browser so your cart survives a page reload. While you fill in a trial application, the basic details (not medical notes) are kept in that browser tab so a reload doesn't lose them; this is deleted when you submit or close the tab. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Other services this website uses

Some photos are loaded from Unsplash, which means your browser connects to their servers. Payments are processed by Paystack. WhatsApp and map links open those services and are covered by their own privacy policies.
